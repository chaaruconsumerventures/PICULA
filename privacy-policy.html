CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

PICULA is a brand operated by CHAARU CONSUMER VENTURES. This policy explains how we handle customer information when you browse our website or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

When you voluntarily send an enquiry or order request, we may receive your name, mobile number, delivery address, city, state, PIN code and selected products.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to customer enquiries.
  • To confirm product availability and order details.
  • To arrange delivery when an order is confirmed.
  • To provide order-related customer support.

3. WhatsApp Order Requests

Our website prepares an order request message. You can review the message before sending it through WhatsApp. An order is not automatically confirmed by opening WhatsApp.

4. Shopping Cart

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember selected cart items. This information remains in your browser unless cleared or changed. The current website does not use a separate customer-account database.

5. Sharing Information

We may share necessary delivery information with courier or logistics providers when arranging a confirmed order. WhatsApp and other service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information. However, no online communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Requests

You may contact us to ask questions about information you have shared, or request correction or deletion where applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website and business services develop.

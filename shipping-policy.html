Shipping Policy

Welcome to PICULA, a brand by CHAARU CONSUMER VENTURES. We aim to provide clear information about dispatch and delivery of your purchases.

1. Order Processing & Dispatch

Once an order is confirmed and payment is received, we aim to dispatch available products within 3–5 working days.

Working days exclude Sundays and applicable public holidays. If an unexpected delay occurs, we will inform you using the contact details provided.

2. Shipping Charges

Product Subtotal Shipping Charge
Below ₹500 ₹60
₹500–₹999 ₹40
₹1,000 and above FREE

Shipping charges are calculated on the product subtotal before shipping.

3. Delivery Time

Delivery time depends on the destination, courier availability and local conditions. Dispatch time does not mean guaranteed delivery time. We will share tracking details when available.

4. Serviceable Locations

We intend to serve customers across India, subject to courier serviceability and applicable business requirements. Availability for a particular PIN code will be confirmed before accepting an order.

5. WhatsApp Order Requests

Submitting a request through WhatsApp does not automatically confirm an order. Product availability, final price, shipping and payment instructions will be confirmed separately by PICULA.

6. Delivery Problems

For damaged, defective or incorrect products, please contact us. You can also read our Return & Refund Policy once published.

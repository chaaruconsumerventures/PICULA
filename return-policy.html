Return & Refund Policy

At PICULA, a brand by CHAARU CONSUMER VENTURES, we aim to provide products that match their descriptions and arrive in good condition.

1. Eligible Returns

We accept return or replacement requests when:

  • The product arrives damaged.
  • The product has a manufacturing defect.
  • An incorrect product is delivered.
  • We send a different size from the one confirmed.
  • The product materially differs from its description.

2. Reporting a Problem

Please contact us as soon as possible after delivery. We request customers to report problems within 48 hours so we can investigate promptly. This request does not limit applicable consumer rights.

3. Photos and Videos

We may request photographs or videos to help understand the issue. These will not be mandatory in every case.

4. Replacement or Refund

For eligible claims, we will offer an appropriate remedy, including replacement or refund, in accordance with applicable law. If a replacement is unavailable, a refund will be provided where appropriate.

5. Return Shipping

For confirmed damaged, defective or incorrect products, PICULA will arrange or cover reasonable return shipping costs where a return is required.

6. Change-of-Mind Returns

We do not currently offer general change-of-mind returns or exchanges. This does not affect remedies available under applicable consumer law.

7. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, we will inform the customer of the refund method and expected processing time. Bank processing times may vary.
